# Patient & Family Portal (`@healthcare-system/patient-web`)

Patient and family member web/PWA application for:
- Email registration and multi-profile consent management (BR-17, BR-20)
- Symptom screening triage (BR-10)
- Advance booking and nurse matching (BR-13)
- Online 30% deposit payments and balance settlement (BR-04)
- Real-time visit tracking and in-app check-in code generation (UC40)
- One-tap Emergency SOS escalation (BR-23)
